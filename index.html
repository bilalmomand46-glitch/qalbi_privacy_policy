Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 12, 2026
Welcome to Qalbi. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and protected when you use the Qalbi mobile application.
Our Commitment: Qalbi is designed to provide Islamic resources and spiritual tools while respecting your privacy. We aim to collect only the information necessary to provide and improve the app's functionality.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, Qalbi may access or process the following types of information:

  • Location Information: Your approximate or precise location may be accessed, with your permission, to calculate accurate prayer times and provide location-based Islamic features.
  • Device Information: Basic technical information, such as device type, operating system version, and app version, may be processed by the app or its service providers.
  • App Preferences: Settings such as language, theme, bookmarks, prayer preferences, and other personalized settings may be stored locally on your device.
  • Usage Information: Information relating to app functionality may be processed by third-party services if those services are integrated into the application.

Qalbi does not require you to provide personal information such as your name, phone number, or email address to use its core features.

2. Location Permissions

Qalbi may request access to your device's location to provide location-dependent features, including:

  • Accurate daily prayer times.
  • Location-based prayer calculations.
  • Qibla direction assistance.

Location permission is requested only when needed for relevant features. You can manage or revoke location permissions at any time through your device settings.

If you deny location access, some location-based features may not function as intended. You may still be able to use supported features by manually selecting a location.

3. How We Use Information

Information accessed or processed through Qalbi may be used for the following purposes:

  • Providing accurate prayer time information.
  • Calculating Qibla direction.
  • Saving bookmarks and personal app preferences.
  • Scheduling prayer and reminder notifications.
  • Providing Quran, Duas, Adhkar, and Tasbeeh features.
  • Improving app performance and reliability.
  • Resolving technical issues and bugs.

4. Data Storage

Certain app preferences and user-generated settings may be stored locally on your device to provide a personalized experience.

This may include bookmarks, selected themes, language preferences, notification settings, and other app-related preferences.

Local data generally remains on your device unless you clear the application data or uninstall the app. Data handled by third-party services is subject to their respective privacy policies.

5. Third-Party Services and APIs

Qalbi may use third-party APIs and services to provide certain features, including Islamic content, prayer time information, and other application functionality.

These services may process technical information or request-related data necessary to respond to your requests.

Third-party service providers operate under their own privacy policies and data protection practices. We encourage users to review the privacy policies of relevant third-party services.

6. Notifications

Qalbi may send local notifications to remind you about prayer times, daily activities, and other features you choose to enable.

These notifications can be managed or disabled at any time through the app settings or your device's notification settings.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Information may be processed or shared with service providers only where necessary to operate application features, provide requested services, maintain functionality, or comply with applicable legal requirements.

We do not intentionally share personal information for unrelated commercial purposes.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through the application. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

We cannot guarantee absolute security, but we aim to use appropriate safeguards to reduce the risk of unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.

9. Children's Privacy

Qalbi is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the application, please contact us so that we can investigate and take appropriate action.

10. Your Privacy Choices

You have control over certain information and permissions associated with Qalbi.

  • You can enable or disable location permissions through your device settings.
  • You can manage notification permissions at any time.
  • You can clear locally stored application data through your device settings.
  • You can uninstall Qalbi to stop using the application.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the application, our practices, or applicable legal requirements.

Any updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date. We encourage users to review this policy periodically.

12. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or how Qalbi handles information, please contact us.
